AUv3 MIDI effect for iPad

The music of the Torah, under your fingers.

Almost every word of the Torah carries a small mark, and each of its 25 marks is a short melody. Torah Tones turns that musical language into MIDI: press a key and the mark's melody plays out in your key, in time with your track.

  • AUM
  • Logic Pro for iPad
  • Loopy Pro
  • Cubasis
  • Drambo
  • Any AUv3 MIDI host

Play the marks

Every mark, one pad, one key

25 pads, grouped the way trope is taught: the lead-in marks together, then each pause with the marks that lead into it. Each pad shows its melody's shape, traced as it plays. On a keyboard the marks run up from C3, one per key.

  • Play several in a row and they wait their turn, so you can chant a whole phrase in time
  • Start on the next 8th or beat, or have the next melody wait for the bar line
  • Hold a key to recite its opening note as long as you like, then let go for the rest
  • Every family plays the chart's own example lines, 41 in all

Any key, any mode

Change the key. Every melody follows.

Each melody is stored as steps of a scale, so it re-sings itself in whatever home note and mode you choose: the melodies as chanted, Major, Minor, Dorian, Harmonic Minor, Lydian, and the Jewish prayer modes Ahavah Rabbah and Mi Sheberach.

  • Change key live with your left hand: one note sets the home note, a chord sets major or minor too
  • Add a harmony line inside the mode, on its own MIDI channel
  • Hold a drone on the home note and fifth that follows every key change

Torah verses

Chant any verse, word by word

Pick any verse of the Five Books by book, chapter and verse, or by weekly portion. Torah Tones chants the passage with each word's melody fitted to its syllables, the last verse closing with the final cadence, while the Hebrew lights up word by word.

  • Play it all in time, or step through it one word per key press
  • Loop the passage on your bar line
  • The English (JPS 1917) sits under every verse

Loop

Marks and rests, in time with your host

Chain marks into a loop that plays when your host's transport runs and locks to its bars. Add rests from half a beat to a bar. The loop ends when its last note does, or rounds up to whole bars if you like.

Feel

Make it breathe

Real chanting isn't quantized. Humanize the timing, velocity and note lengths, add rubato that leans into the end of each phrase, and a cantor's grace notes. Turn melodies upside down or backwards, at half or double speed.

Where it fits

Torah Tones makes no sound itself. It sends MIDI, so it sings with whatever instrument you put after it: a synth, a sampler, a piano, a shofar.

Your keyboard or the pads→Torah Tones→Any instrument

Load it as a MIDI effect in AUM, Logic Pro for iPad, Loopy Pro, Cubasis or Drambo. On its own, the app sends to a MIDI input called “Torah Tones” that other apps can pick, and to connected keyboards and synths. GarageBand doesn't host MIDI effects.
