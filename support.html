Support

Torah Tones is an AUv3 MIDI effect for iPad that plays the melodies of Torah chanting (trope) as MIDI notes, in any key and mode, in time with your music app. Here are answers to the usual questions. Anything else: write, and a person answers.

Questions

I tap a pad and hear nothing.
Torah Tones makes no sound itself; it sends MIDI. Put an instrument after it: in AUM or Logic Pro, add an instrument, then add Torah Tones as a MIDI effect in front of it. Using the app on its own, open another music app and choose “Torah Tones” as its MIDI input. (The Hear buttons are the only places it plays sound itself, through the iPad’s speaker: on the Learn Trope page, and “Hear a phrase” in the welcome. Everything else is MIDI.)
Which apps can host it?
Any app that hosts AUv3 MIDI effects on iPad, including AUM, Logic Pro for iPad, Loopy Pro, Cubasis and Drambo. GarageBand doesn't host MIDI effects.
Which keys play which marks?
The 25 marks run up from C3, one per key, and every pad shows its key. Assign MIDI on the Pads page puts any mark on any key: tap a pad, then press the key you want (or use All Marks to move them with − and +); MIDI Setup moves them all together, or learns the starting key from your keyboard. Keys are saved with presets and with your host's project. Keys below that are your left hand.
How do I change the key while I play?
Use the home note arrows at the top, or play with your left hand: one key sets the home note, and a chord can set major or minor too (MIDI Setup › Left hand).
Why does Etnachta play three notes on one pad and two on another setting?
A mark's tune depends on the word: one steady note for each syllable before the accent, then the melody. “Before the accent” on the Pads page chooses: as on the mark's own name (et-nach-ta), none, or one to three.
Can it follow my song's tempo?
Yes. In a host it follows the host's tempo and bars. Feel › Next melody starts can make each melody wait for the next beat or bar line.
Can I set the tempo without a host?
Yes. Using the app on its own, tap the tempo in the row under the tabs: step it with − and +, drag the slider, or tap the beat. In a host, the host's tempo wins.
Can I put the pads in my own order?
Yes. On the Pads page, choose My Layout, tap Rearrange, and drag a pad: a dashed space shows where it will land. “Start from…” begins from the family or keyboard order. Tap Done when it’s right.
How do presets work?
Presets keep everything as it is now: the key and mode, Feel, MIDI channels, the sequence and the verses you chose. Tap Presets to save, load, rename or delete. In AUM or Logic Pro the same presets show in the host's own preset menu, and the host also saves the plugin's settings inside your project.
Can I get the notes into my DAW?
Yes. Save as MIDI File, on the Sequence page (once, or 2, 4 or 8 times through) and on the Torah verses page, writes a standard MIDI file at your tempo, in your key and mode, with your Feel settings. Save it to Files and open it in Logic, GarageBand, Cubasis or any DAW.
Which trope does it use?
Reform Torah cantillation, as charted in The Art of Torah Cantillation. There are many traditions; this is one of them.
New to trope?
Open Guide at the top of the screen, then Learn Trope. To learn to chant it yourself, Torah: Turn It teaches it lesson by lesson.
